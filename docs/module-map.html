The shape of it

Five layers, top to bottom: the app boots, GetX decides which of 3 top-level screens to show, that screen is one of 8 feature modules, modules read from 3 services, services are built from 4 plain data models. The one thing this shape doesn't tell you on its own — flagged directly on the diagram — is that footprint isn't purely "above" home and scanner: it depends on both of them right back.
